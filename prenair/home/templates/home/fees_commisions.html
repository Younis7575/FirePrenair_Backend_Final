Fees & Commissions

At FirePrenair, we are committed to maintaining transparency in our fee structure, ensuring that both sellers and buyers benefit from a fair and straightforward system.

For Sellers: Commission Rates

  • WorkPrenair: 17% commission on successfully completed projects.
  • DigiPrenair: 12% commission on each digital product sold.
  • EduPrenair: 20% commission on course enrollments or subscriptions.
  • CommuPrenair: 15% commission on ticketed events, premium memberships, or networking services.

Additional Seller Fees

  • Payment Processing Fee: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction (varies by payment gateway).
  • Currency Conversion Fee: 2-3% fee for cross-border sales.

For Buyers: Transaction Fee

Buyers are charged a 5% transaction fee per purchase, supporting platform maintenance and security.

What These Fees Cover

  • For Sellers: Access to a global marketplace, AI-powered tools, and analytics to track performance.
  • For Buyers: Secure payment gateways, quality assurance, and buyer protection.

How Fees Are Calculated and Displayed

  • Sellers: Fees are deducted from earnings and detailed in the "Earnings Summary" dashboard.
  • Buyers: Fees are transparently displayed during checkout.

Why FirePrenair Fees are Competitive

  • Global opportunities across 50+ countries.
  • Value-driven services with premium tools.
  • Transparent system with no hidden fees.

For further assistance or inquiries, reach out to our support team at support@fireprenair.com.
